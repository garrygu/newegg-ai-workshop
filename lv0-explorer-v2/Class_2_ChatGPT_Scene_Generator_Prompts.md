# 🤖 ChatGPT Prompt Templates for Part 2: Scene Generation

**Purpose:** Help students break down their story into 3-5 well-structured scenes

---

## 📋 When to Use This

**Use ChatGPT to help with:**
- Breaking your story into 3-5 scenes
- Defining WHO, WHERE, WHAT, MOOD for each scene
- Getting scene duration recommendations
- Refining your scene descriptions

**Don't use it for:**
- Writing your entire story idea (do that yourself!)
- Creating AI image prompts (you'll learn that in Part 3!)

---

## 🎯 Option A: Using a Story Template

**If you chose one of the preset story templates (Lost Robot, Animal Power, Time Traveler), use this:**

### Copy and paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I'm working on a kids' story for a video project. I need to break it down into 3-5 scenes for a storyboard.

Story Template: [CHOOSE ONE: Lost Robot / Animal with Secret Power / Time Traveler]

My customizations:
- Character name: [YOUR CHARACTER NAME]
- Unique detail 1: [YOUR CUSTOMIZATION]
- Unique detail 2: [YOUR CUSTOMIZATION]
- Setting preference: [ANY SPECIFIC LOCATIONS YOU WANT]

Beginning: [BRIEFLY DESCRIBE YOUR BEGINNING]
Middle: [BRIEFLY DESCRIBE YOUR MIDDLE]
End: [BRIEFLY DESCRIBE YOUR END]

Please create 3-5 scenes for my storyboard. For each scene, provide:

1. Scene number
2. WHO: Which characters are present
3. WHERE: Location, time of day, weather/atmosphere
4. WHAT: What's happening (action and emotion)
5. MOOD: Overall feeling/atmosphere
6. DURATION: Recommended length (aim for 15-30 seconds per scene)

Requirements:
- Maximum 5 scenes total
- Each scene should be 15-30 seconds
- Total duration should be 60-120 seconds
- Clear beginning → middle → end story arc
- Main character should appear in most scenes
- Each scene should have ONE main action or emotion

Format your response as a table or clearly numbered list.
```

### Example (Lost Robot Template):

```
I'm working on a kids' story for a video project. I need to break it down into 3-5 scenes for a storyboard.

Story Template: Lost Robot

My customizations:
- Character name: Spark
- Unique detail 1: Has one glowing antenna that changes color with emotions
- Unique detail 2: Gets lost during a thunderstorm
- Setting preference: Dark forest, then a robot village

Beginning: Spark gets lost in a storm and feels scared
Middle: A kind girl named Luna helps Spark find the way home
End: Spark reunites with robot family, everyone is happy

Please create 3-5 scenes for my storyboard. For each scene, provide:
[rest of prompt as above...]
```

## 🎯 Option B: Custom Story

**If you're creating your own story, use this:**

### Copy and paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I'm working on a kids' story for a video project. I need to break it down into 3-5 scenes for a storyboard.

My Story:

Character: [DESCRIBE YOUR MAIN CHARACTER]
Genre/Theme: [e.g., adventure, friendship, mystery, fantasy]

Beginning (Setup): [DESCRIBE WHAT HAPPENS AT THE START]

Middle (Conflict/Challenge): [DESCRIBE THE MAIN PROBLEM OR ADVENTURE]

End (Resolution): [DESCRIBE HOW IT ENDS]

Please create 3-5 scenes for my storyboard. For each scene, provide:

1. Scene number
2. WHO: Which characters are present
3. WHERE: Location, time of day, weather/atmosphere
4. WHAT: What's happening (action and emotion)
5. MOOD: Overall feeling/atmosphere
6. DURATION: Recommended length (aim for 15-30 seconds per scene)

Requirements:
- Maximum 5 scenes total
- Each scene should be 15-30 seconds
- Total duration should be 60-120 seconds
- Clear beginning → middle → end story arc
- Main character should appear in most scenes
- Each scene should have ONE main action or emotion
- Keep it simple - this is for a short video (1-2 minutes total)

Format your response as a table or clearly numbered list.
```

## 🔄 Refining Your Scenes

**If ChatGPT's suggestions are too complex or too simple, use this follow-up:**

### Copy and paste this:

```
Thank you! The scenes look [too complex / too simple / good but need adjustments].

Can you:
- [CHOOSE WHAT YOU NEED:]
  □ Simplify the scenes - make them easier to visualize
  □ Add more detail to scene [X]
  □ Combine scenes [X] and [Y] into one scene
  □ Split scene [X] into two separate scenes
  □ Make the mood more [happy/sad/exciting/mysterious]
  □ Reduce to only 3 scenes (keep the most important moments)

Keep the same format with WHO, WHERE, WHAT, MOOD, and DURATION.
```

## 📊 What Good Output Looks Like

### Example ChatGPT Response:

```
Here are 3 scenes for your "Spark the Lost Robot" story:

SCENE 1: Lost in the Storm (20 seconds)
- WHO: Spark (alone)
- WHERE: Dark forest at night, heavy rain falling
- WHAT: Spark is standing alone, looking scared and confused, looking up at the stormy sky
- MOOD: Sad, lonely, frightening
- DURATION: 20 seconds

SCENE 2: A Friendly Face (25 seconds)
- WHO: Spark and Luna (girl in red raincoat)
- WHERE: Forest path, rain is stopping, golden light breaking through clouds
- WHAT: Luna kneels down, reaches out her hand to Spark with a gentle smile; Spark looks up with hopeful eyes
- MOOD: Warm, hopeful, kind
- DURATION: 25 seconds

SCENE 3: Home at Last (25 seconds)
- WHO: Spark, Luna, Spark's robot family (mother and sibling robots)
- WHERE: Colorful robot village with dome-shaped houses, sunny day, rainbow in sky
- WHAT: Spark hugs robot family; Luna waves goodbye with a big smile
- MOOD: Joyful, happy, heartwarming
- DURATION: 25 seconds

TOTAL DURATION: 70 seconds
```

**You would then copy this information into your worksheet!**

## ✅ How to Use the Output

### Step 1: Review ChatGPT's Response

Ask yourself:
- ✅ Do I have 3-5 scenes?
- ✅ Is each scene 15-30 seconds?
- ✅ Does it tell a complete story (beginning → middle → end)?
- ✅ Can I VISUALIZE each scene clearly?
- ✅ Is my main character in most scenes?

### Step 2: Customize if Needed

**You can:**
- Change character names
- Adjust locations
- Modify emotions/moods
- Request changes (see "Refining Your Scenes" above)

### Step 3: Transfer to Your Worksheet

**Copy each scene into Part 2 of your worksheet:**
- WHO → Copy to WHO field
- WHERE → Copy to WHERE field
- WHAT → Copy to WHAT field
- MOOD → Copy to MOOD field
- DURATION → Copy to Duration field

## ⚠️ Important Reminders

### What ChatGPT CAN Help With:
✅ Breaking down your story into scenes
✅ Suggesting scene details (WHO, WHERE, WHAT, MOOD)
✅ Recommending durations
✅ Refining and adjusting scenes

❌ Add art style information
❌ Create your Character Memory Pack

### Remember:
- Scene descriptions ≠ AI prompts
- You'll convert scenes → prompts in Part 3
- ChatGPT is helping with PLANNING, not prompt engineering

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:

1. **Be specific about your customizations**
   - Don't just say "a robot" - say "a small blue robot with glowing eyes"

2. **Mention if you have location preferences**
   - "I want one scene in a classroom" → ChatGPT will include it

3. **Tell ChatGPT your story's tone**
   - "This is a happy story" vs "This is a spooky mystery"

4. **Ask for adjustments**
   - Don't settle for the first response if it doesn't feel right

5. **Keep it visual**
   - Ask ChatGPT: "Is this easy to visualize as an image?"
