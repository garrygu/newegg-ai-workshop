# 🌟 AI Workshop - Explorer Level (V3)

Welcome to the **Explorer Level** of the AI Workshop curriculum! This level uses **project-based learning** where students plan THEIR story first, then learn AI tools to bring their vision to life. No coding experience needed!

## 📚 Curriculum Ladder

```
Explorer → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert
         ↑ YOU ARE HERE!
```

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, you will:
- ✨ Understand what AI is and how it's used in daily life
- 🎨 Create images using AI text-to-image tools
- 🎬 Make videos and voiceovers with AI
- 📖 Build your own AI-powered story project
- 🤔 Think critically about AI ethics and creativity

## 📓 Workshop Structure (3 Classes)

Complete these classes in order:

### Class 1: 🌍 [Discover AI Around You](./1_Discover_AI_Around_You.ipynb)
**Duration:** 2 hours (including 10-15 min break)

- Learn what AI is through fun examples
- Discover AI in everyday life
- Try ChatGPT hands-on
- Explore AI translation and deepfakes
- Discuss AI ethics and "AI for Good"
- Reflect on AI's impact

**Status:** ✅ **Complete**

---

### Class 2: 📖 [Plan Your Story & Create with AI](./2_Plan_and_Create_Your_Story.ipynb)
**Duration:** 2.5-3 hours (including 10-15 min break)

**Part 1: Story Planning (30 min)**
- Plan YOUR complete story idea
- Create detailed storyboard (3-5 scenes)
- Use ChatGPT to help with story development

**Part 2: Learn AI Image Creation (30 min)**
- Learn about text-to-image AI
- Practice writing effective prompts
- Understand the prompt recipe

**Part 3: Create Images for YOUR Story (60 min)**
- Generate AI images for each scene in YOUR storyboard
- Organize files for Class 3

**BONUS (if time):** 🎵 AI Music Generation
- Create custom background music for your story
- Tools: Suno AI, Soundraw, or AIVA
- Perfect for students who finish early!

**Goal:** Complete story plan + all images ready for animation!

---

### Class 3: 🎬 [Animate, Voice & Assemble Your Story](./3_Animate_Voice_and_Assemble_Your_Story.ipynb)
**Duration:** 2.5-3 hours (including 10-15 min break)

**Part 1: AI Video Animation (45 min)**
- Learn Runway ML to animate your images
- Create 2-3 animated video clips (optional)

**Part 2: Assemble Your Story in Canva (90 min)** ⭐ Main focus!
- Upload all assets (images + videos)
- **Use Canva's built-in AI text-to-speech** for voice narration
- Add transitions and polish
- Export final video

**Part 3: Showcase (20 min)**
- Present your story to the class
- Celebrate and reflect!

**BONUS (if time):**
- Professional AI voices with ElevenLabs (if you want ultra-realistic voices)

**Goal:** Complete 1-2 minute AI-generated video story!

## 📊 What's New in V3?

### Key Philosophy: **Project-Based Learning**
Students plan THEIR story first, then learn each tool as they need it to build their vision!

### Key Improvements:
- ✅ **Streamlined from 4 to 3 classes** - More focused, better pacing
- ✅ **Story planning comes FIRST** - Students are motivated to learn tools for THEIR project
- ✅ **Class 2: Plan + Create Images** - Story planning & storyboarding, then image creation
- ✅ **Class 3: Animate + Voice + Assemble** - All remaining tools learned to complete the story
- ✅ **Bonus modules separated** - Optional activities don't slow down core learning
- ✅ **Clearer tool recommendations** - One primary tool per category
- ✅ **Better time management** - Realistic 2.5-3 hour sessions

### Time Comparison:
- **V1:** 4 classes (2h + 1h + 2h + 2h = 7 hours)
- **V3:** 3 classes (2h + 2.5h + 2.5h = 7 hours)
- Same total time, better structure! ⚡

### Pedagogical Advantage:
Learning tools IN SERVICE of creating YOUR story is more engaging than learning tools first, then deciding what to make!
