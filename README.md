# 🎓 Newegg AI Workshop - Multi-Level Program

> **Empowering the next generation of AI creators through progressive, hands-on learning**

---

## 🗺️ **Program Overview**

This repository contains a comprehensive, multi-level AI education program designed for learners of all backgrounds and experience levels. From complete beginners to advanced practitioners, our curriculum progressively builds AI knowledge and skills through engaging, project-based workshops.

---

## 📚 **Program Levels**

Our program is organized into five progressive levels, each designed to build upon previous knowledge:

| Level | Identity | Sessions | Age | Description |
|:--:|:--|:--:|:--:|:--|
| **🌱 Explorer** | *AI Storyteller* | 3 | 10–14 | Create AI-powered stories with no-code tools |
| **🚀 Beginner** | *AI Builder* | 6 | 13–16 | First coding experience building AI projects |
| **⚡ Intermediate** | *AI System Builder* | 6–8 | 15+ | Design domain-aware AI systems with RAG |
| **🔥 Advanced** | *Production AI Engineer* | 8–10 | 16+ | Deploy, monitor, and secure AI applications |
| **💎 Expert** | *AI Researcher* | 10–12 | 17+ | Research literacy and open-source contribution |

---

## 🎯 **Getting Started**

### **Choose Your Level**

1. **New to AI?** Start with [Explorer Level](lv0-explorer-v2/)
   - No coding required
   - Creative and fun activities
   - Perfect for beginners

2. **Ready to code?** Begin with [Beginner Level](lv1-beginner-v2/)
   - No programming experience needed
   - Step-by-step Python tutorials
   - Build your first AI project

3. **Already coding?** Check your skills:
   - Know basic Python? → Start at [Beginner Level](lv1-beginner-v2/) to learn AI concepts
   - Confident with Python? → Try Intermediate Level *(coming soon)*
   - Have AI/ML experience? → Jump to Advanced or Expert levels *(coming soon)*

### **Each Level Contains:**
- 📖 **README.md** - Overview and getting started guide
- 📓 **Session Notebooks** - Interactive Jupyter notebooks with lessons
- 🎯 **Projects** - Hands-on projects to build
- 📚 **Documentation** - Additional resources and guides (where applicable)
- 👨‍🏫 **Instructor Materials** - Teaching guides for educators (where applicable)

## 🏗️ **Curriculum Structure**

Each level is designed as a complete workshop series:

- **3–12 sessions** (2 hours each, varies by level)
- **Progressive skill building** from exploration to research contribution
- **Capstone projects** at every level for portfolio building
- **Clear scope boundaries** — explicit goals and non-goals per level
