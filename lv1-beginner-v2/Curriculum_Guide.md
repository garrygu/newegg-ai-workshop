# 📚 Curriculum Guide for Parents and Students

> **Understanding the Newegg AI Foundations Workshop v2**

---

## 🎯 Workshop Overview

The **Newegg AI Foundations Workshop v2** is a comprehensive 7-session program designed to introduce high school students to artificial intelligence through hands-on coding projects. This enhanced version includes modern AI topics like Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering.

### Who This Is For
- **High school students** (ages 13-18)
- **No programming experience required**
- **Curious about AI and technology**

### What You'll Build
By the end, you'll have created:
- 🤖 **Your own AI assistant** using ChatGPT/Gemini APIs
- 🎨 **AI-generated artwork** with Stable Diffusion
- 🧠 **Image recognition AI** trained by you
- 💬 **Talking chatbot** that understands emotions
- 🎮 **Complete AI game** combining everything!

---

## 📖 Session Overview

| Session | Topic | Duration | Key Skills |
|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| **0** | Setup & AI Fundamentals | 2 hrs | Python basics, AI concepts |
| **1** | Prompt Engineering & LLMs | 2 hrs | ChatGPT API, prompt techniques |
| **2** | AI Image Generation | 2 hrs | Stable Diffusion, creative prompts |
| **3** | Image Classification | 2 hrs | Neural networks, training |
| **4** | Chatbot, Sentiment & Voice | 2 hrs | NLP, optional voice AI |
| **5** | AI Game + Agents | 2 hrs | Integration, showcase |
| **6** | The Ultimate AI Game | 2 hrs | ResNet, LLMs, Voice AI, Gradio UI |

**Total: 14 hours of hands-on learning**
