# 🚀 YouthAI Complete Learning Path (Lv0–Lv4)

Your journey from AI **user** to AI **creator** starts here. 👇

---

## 🌱 Level 0 — Explorer

### *AI Storyteller*

**Recommended Age:** 10–14
**Sessions:** 3
**Big Idea:**

> *"AI isn't magic — it's a creative tool you can use right now."*

### What You'll Learn

* How AI generates text, images, and audio
* How to give AI good instructions (prompts)
* How to create a story using AI tools

### What You'll Build

📖 **An AI-powered story** with:
* AI-written script
* AI-generated images
* AI voiceover (optional)

### After Lv0, You Can Say:

> "I used AI to create something original — and I understand how it works."

---

## 🚀 Level 1 — Beginner

### *AI Builder*

**Recommended Age:** 13–16
**Sessions:** 6
**Big Idea:**

> *"Writing code to control AI gives you real superpowers."*

### What You'll Learn

* Python basics (variables, loops, functions)
* How to talk to AI through code (APIs)
* How to generate images with prompts
* How computers recognize images
* How chatbots understand emotions

### What You'll Build

🎮 **An AI Guessing Game** that:
* Generates images from prompts
* Classifies what's in pictures
* Chats with you and understands your mood
* (Optional) Talks and listens using voice

### After Lv1, You Can Say:

> "I can write Python code to build real AI projects."

---

## 🧩 Level 2 — Intermediate

### *AI System Builder*

**Recommended Age:** 15+
**Sessions:** 6–8
**Big Idea:**

> *"AI should know things, use tools, and answer better — not just guess."*

### What You'll Learn

In Lv2, you learn how to build **smarter AI systems**, not just chatbots.

You'll learn how to:

* Help AI **look things up** instead of hallucinating
* Store information using **embeddings**
* Build AI that answers questions from **your own data**
* Control how much information AI sees
* Understand *why* AI answers go wrong

You'll also *get a taste of*:

* AI tools & simple agents
* Customizing models (intro only)
* AI that understands images + text (optional)

### What You'll Build

🎓 **A real AI assistant** that:

* Knows a specific topic (school, products, documents, etc.)
* Uses its own knowledge base
* Explains answers clearly and responsibly

### After Lv2, You Can Say:

> "I can design AI systems that actually know what they're talking about."

---

## ⚙️ Level 3 — Advanced

### *Production AI Engineer*

**Recommended Age:** 16+
**Sessions:** 8–10
**Big Idea:**

> *"AI that works once is easy. AI that works reliably is hard."*

### What You'll Learn

Lv3 is about making AI **real-world ready**.

You'll learn how to:

* Understand how large AI models work (no heavy math)
* Train and improve models more efficiently
* Run AI apps that other people can actually use
* Track experiments and results
* Protect AI systems from misuse or attacks

You'll also explore:

* Multi-step AI reasoning
* Simple multi-agent systems
* AI safety and security basics

### What You'll Build

🛠️ **A deployed AI app** that:

* Runs through a web interface
* Is monitored and logged
* Has basic safety protections
* Can explain how it works and where it can fail

### After Lv3, You Can Say:

> "I can build AI systems that are safe, reliable, and production-ready."

---

## 🧪 Level 4 — Expert

### *AI Researcher & Open-Source Contributor*

**Recommended Age:** 17+
**Sessions:** 10–12
**Big Idea:**

> *"How do new AI ideas get created — and how do we improve them responsibly?"*

### What You'll Learn

Lv4 is about **thinking like an AI researcher**.

You'll learn how to:

* Train small AI models from scratch
* Understand how AI alignment works (ethics & control)
* Explore image and video generation
* Read real AI research papers
* Reproduce and test AI experiments
* Contribute to open-source AI projects

You'll also explore:

* Diffusion models
* Video AI concepts
* Robotics & simulation (intro level)
* Responsible AI release practices

### What You'll Build

🧠 **Choose one:**

* Your own mini AI research project
* A reproduction of a real research paper
* A contribution to an open-source AI project

You'll present:

* What you tried
* What worked
* What didn't
* What you learned

### After Lv4, You Can Say:

> "I understand how AI research works — and I can contribute to it."

| Coding     | No code   | Notebooks | Scripts        | Packages       | Libraries        |
| Deployment | None      | Local     | Demo apps      | Docker         | Kubernetes       |
